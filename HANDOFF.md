## 1. Product

AllShip AI is a US freight and moving marketplace, rebuilt from scratch as **one Next.js project** (`allship-ai`). It replaces three old repos:
- `allship-be`: Express + Mongo.
- `allshipai-da`: dashboard.
- `allshipai-la`: marketing landing site, built by a marketing team.

**Roles:**
- **Shipper:** posts loads.
- **Carrier:** a company organization. Its members are an **owner**, **dispatchers** and **drivers**.
- **Admin:** platform staff.

**Core flow:**
1. A shipper posts a load. There are 23 categories with typed forms.
2. Verified carriers bid.
3. The shipper accepts a bid and pays into **Stripe Connect escrow**: the carrier's bid plus a 10% platform fee. The carrier keeps 100% of the bid (`PLATFORM_FEE_BPS=1000`).
4. The carrier assigns a driver.
5. The driver starts the trip (live GPS), confirms pickup with photos, then confirms delivery with POD photos and a signature.
6. The shipper confirms delivery, or it auto-completes after 72h. The payout is then transferred to the carrier.
7. Then reviews, or a dispute if something went wrong.

**AI assistant:** chat that uses OpenAI tools and helps shippers, carriers and drivers. It can draft loads and bids and start guided tours (Driver.js). It never mutates data itself; the user confirms every action.

**Market:** US, with i18n prep (`user.locale` exists, no translations yet).

**Tips**
- **Sign-in returns 429:** clear the local `rate_limit` table.
- **Separate production builds:** `NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-x` is supported (`next.config.ts` `distDir`). Remove any `.next-x` entries the build adds to `tsconfig.json` and `next-env.d.ts`.
- **Adding CSP-sensitive code:**
  - A new inline script in the root layout must be added to `layoutInlineScripts` (`src/lib/security/inline-scripts.ts`).
  - A new dynamic surface must be added to `NONCE_ROUTE_PREFIXES`, and the proxy matcher must cover it with anchored segments.
