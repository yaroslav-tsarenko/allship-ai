## Bot sign-up attack (Feb 26–27, 2026)
- 34,921 accounts in ~36 hours, one every ~2.5 s, all phones starting `190…`, none verified, real-looking webmail addresses (gmail 85%).
- The old `/auth/register-and-auth` had no rate limit and no captcha and sends a verification email on every call, so the most likely purpose was **email bombing through AllShip's sender** (each victim address got a "Verify your AllShipAI account" email). This can hurt the sending domain's reputation.
- The rebuild already covers this: Turnstile + per-IP/per-email rate limits on sign-up, and verification email throttling.

## Real users (17)
6 carriers (with company name, USDOT/MC on 6, rate tiers on 5), 1 driver, 1 admin, 9 customers (4 verified). 7 accounts have plain-text passwords (old driver/password-change bugs).

## Migration decision (proposed)
| Data | Action |
|---|---|
| 17 real users | migrate; bcrypt hashes kept as legacy and rehashed on first login; 7 plain-text passwords dropped → forced reset |
| 6 carriers | migrate to organizations + carrier_profile (FMCSA status `unverified`, must re-verify) |
| 34,921 bot accounts | **do not migrate** |
| loads (53) + 2 real bids | do not migrate to production (52 are orphaned test loads); use as anonymized demo seed for dev/preview |
| AI bids, AI reviews | do not migrate |
| chats, aichats, helpquotes | do not migrate (test content) |
| transactions | do not migrate (fake confirmations) |
| cards | **never migrate**; recommend deleting `test.cards` in Atlas yourself after the rebuild goes live |

Result: production starts almost clean, with real accounts carried over.
